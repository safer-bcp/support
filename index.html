Available Support Services

If you need help, you are not alone. These services are here for you.

Emergency 999

For non-emergencies call 101. If you call 999 but cannot speak safely, listen to the operator’s questions and, from a mobile, press 55 when prompted to be transferred to the police.

  • The Shores (Dorset SARC)

    Support for anyone who has experienced rape or sexual assault, recent or not. You do not have to report to the police.

    Open 24 hours, every day

    the-shores.org.uk
    0800 970 9954

  • STARS Dorset

    Local Dorset support for survivors of sexual violence, of any age or gender. Counselling, ISVA support and referrals.

    Mon 8.30am–5pm · Tue–Thu 8.30am–7pm · Fri 8.30am–4pm · Closed Sat & Sun

    starsdorset.org
    01202 308840

  • 24/7 Rape & Sexual Abuse Support Line

    Free, confidential support from Rape Crisis for anyone aged 16+ affected by rape, sexual assault, abuse or harassment. Online chat also available.

    Open 24 hours, every day

    247sexualabusesupport.org.uk
    0808 500 2222

  • National Domestic Abuse Helpline

    Free, confidential support and safety planning for anyone experiencing domestic abuse, run by Refuge.

    Open 24 hours, every day

    nationaldahelpline.org.uk
    0808 2000 247

  • Shout

    The UK’s free, confidential mental health text service. Text the word SHOUT to start a conversation.

    Open 24 hours, every day

    giveusashout.org
    Text 85258

  • Strut Safe

    A friendly voice on the phone to keep you company while you walk home alone, until you are through the door.

    Thu 7pm–1am · Fri & Sat 7pm–3am · Sun 7pm–1am

    strutsafe.org
    0333 335 0026

Covering your tracks online

If you are worried that someone is monitoring your device, consider using a phone or computer they cannot access, such as one at a library. You can also delete this page from your browser history, or open it in a private or incognito window next time.
